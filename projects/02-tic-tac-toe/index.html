GridDuel

Two players, one board. The winner and the draw are derived from the squares array on every render, not stored in state.

Based on the freeCodeCamp "Build a Tic-Tac-Toe Game" certification project (Front-End Development Libraries v9). The game logic follows the platform's requirements; the interface, the scoreboard and the highlighted winning line were designed for this page.
