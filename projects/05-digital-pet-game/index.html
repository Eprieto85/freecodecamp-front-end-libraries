PixelPal

Name your pet and keep it happy. Its mood is computed from Hunger, Happiness and Energy with a typed PetMood enum and a Record of emojis.

Based on the freeCodeCamp "Build a Digital Pet Game" certification project (Front-End Development Libraries v9). The requirements and the mood rules follow the platform's project; the interface and the progress bars were designed for this page. Source in index.tsx, compiled to script.js.
