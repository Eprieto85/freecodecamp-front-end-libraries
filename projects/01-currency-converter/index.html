ExchangeLab

Convert between four currencies. The conversion table is memoized: it is recalculated only when the amount or the source currency changes, never when you change the target.

Based on the freeCodeCamp "Build a Currency Converter" certification project (Front-End Development Libraries v9). The requirements and the memoization logic follow the platform's project; the interface, the swap button and the rates table were designed for this page.
