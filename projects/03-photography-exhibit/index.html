Lumina Gallery

Three landscapes, one grid. The whole layout is built with Tailwind utility classes only — there is no custom CSS file.

Colosseo

An ancient amphitheatre in the heart of Rome, glowing in the late afternoon light.

Alps

Snowy peaks catching the first light of the day above a sea of clouds.

Sea

A calm morning by the coast, where the water meets the horizon.

Based on the freeCodeCamp "Design a Photography Exhibit" certification project (Front-End Development Libraries v9). The required structure (grid container, cards, subheadings, descriptions) follows the platform's project; the dark gallery design, hover effects and responsive layout were designed for this page.
